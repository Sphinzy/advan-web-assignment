About Us

"JOBHUB"

"JOBHUB" or FYC, derived from "For Youth Cambodia", was created to assist Cambodian students in selecting suitable career paths to prevent them from dropping out of studies. We also provide scholarships for talented or underprivileged students, and opportunities for students to gain work experience according to their skills.

Career Guidance

Students can explore various fields and universities to make informed decisions about their preferred careers.

Scholarships

Numerous scholarships are available for talented or underprivileged students eager to continue their education.

Job Opportunities

We provide various job opportunities to help students find work easily across multiple industries.

Events

Our events share information about volunteer work and professional opportunities.

Our Mission

To help students make informed career choices by providing comprehensive information on all fields, universities in Cambodia, and numerous scholarships for every student.

Our Vision

To support all students in making career choices by offering guidance across multiple fields, universities in Cambodia, and access to numerous scholarships.

We Value

Clarity

We simplify complex career paths and provide clear, concise, and easy-to-understand information.

Personalization

We provide personalized guidance to help each individual find a future aligned with their values, interests, and unique skills.

Integrity

We offer guidance and information based on accurate data, free from external influence.

Accessibility

We make our resources available to all students, regardless of background, location, or socioeconomic status.

This platform provides career guidance, job information, scholarships, and opportunities JOBHUB to make informed decisions about their future.
